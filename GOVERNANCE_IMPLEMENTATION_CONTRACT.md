# Governance and ministry operations — implementation contract

Status: additive foundation only; existing Worker/UI is not integrated. Do not deploy before regression and live authorization testing. Do not commit private member profiles, photos, contact details, or original private source documents. Preserve all existing records and modules.

## Authorization
Separate persistent or optionally expiring Permission from mandatory time-bounded Delegation. Director alone creates/revokes either. Delegation requires recipient account, issuer, purpose, module/action/scope, start/end, reference, status, acceptance, letter, audit history, deliverables and closure. Expiration enforced at every server read/write/export, not by client or a scheduled job alone. Preserve independent base grants. Never delegate granting authority, director identity, passwords, secrets or unrestricted clinical records. Reconcile old fullAdmin flags explicitly; never migrate them silently into director roles. All HQ leaders may receive explicitly scoped access across operational modules. Test direct API requests, stale sessions, offline data and expiry. Existing staff/HQ shared reporting policy retained unless explicitly changed. Verify role list discrepancy: manual has eight HQ departments, existing model adds Monday Program, legacy event_leader needs director classification. Do not assume dual-role membership without approved assignments.

## Center directory and member lifecycle
Use stable IDs, direction + approved display name, editable A/B/C level with decision history, active status, leader and department assignments. Do not invent live data or silently seed ambiguous center names. Member registry distinct from login accounts: optional private consent-based photo, name, age band, contact as necessary, church, center, batch, join date, membership start, exit/transfer date and reason, mentor, development level 1/2/3, stage label, status history, scheduled review, expected exit, elapsed duration. Guest arrivals and retention measured from first/last visit and repeat attendance; potential members and potential leaders are separate concepts. Track active members, guests, potential members, volunteers, interns, potential leaders, alumni, joiners, departures, transitions, retention with explicit denominators. Do not automatically rank spiritual worth by duration. Manual 42-month membership reference, 18-month leadership eligibility and 24-month tenure; flag exceptions for review. Center capacity 12 founding, 120 ordinary, up to 200 with HQ approval. Do not infer real current totals. Youth photo access private, consent and where applicable guardian approval, no public exports, no offline cache, retention/deletion policy. Aggregate reporting for broader authorized audiences.

## Center leaders
Add center_senior_leader to registration, role editing and backend. Approved center leader roles require a department and mentorship responsibility; potential leaders exempt. One account, multiple assignments. Mentees linked by stable ID, names and count derived from active links; allow zero with unassigned flag. Mentor sees own mentees, directors and authorized senior leaders operational summary; sensitive details separately restricted. Sunday and other center meetings attendance only for assigned center leadership roles; Monday program/attendance only staff and directors. Separate session ID, program type, date, center ID, participant, status; prevent duplicates and cross-center records. No self-approval. Confirm recording authority and whether self-check-in is allowed before enabling.

## Plans and reporting
Every HQ department, center and center department gets its own six-month plan and monthly milestones, owner, objective, activity, KPI, baseline, target, actual, budget, expenditure, evidence, challenges (explicit none allowed), causes, action, deadline, quantitative and qualitative results, decisions and feedback. Scope by center + department stable IDs. Draft private to owner, submitted/approved center summaries shared in same center, directors all, HQ Coordination only as granted. Charter content accessible to directors and responsible department leaders. Coordination reviews submissions and tracks overdue reports; director approval or explicit review/approval delegation. Preserve original plans and revisions. Three-month review and six-month closeout. Include prayers, collaboration, gift development and external partnership approval per manual.

## Director workspaces
Track own six-month plan separately, all HQ activities and participation, follow-up of monthly reports, center plans and approval via Coordination, successors (two potential successors), alumni and consent-based contact, annual unity event and celebration, fundraising pipeline and finance growth, quarterly special staff meeting, individual youth/leader formal and informal meetings/visits, domestic relations and partnerships, youth opportunities and resources, community programs, quantitative/qualitative evaluation, media/admin oversight. Preserve personal director plan privacy and existing private import. Recurrences: one advisory member meeting monthly; one visit to each Addis center and monthly Addis leaders meeting; Bishoftu every 14 days; quarterly special staff meeting; annual unity and celebration. Record scheduled and completed occurrences separately, reschedule with reason, action owner and due date. No fabricated completion.

## Document committee
Committee membership and role-based access, document registry, owner, version history, review assignments, internal comments, advisory request (approved excerpt), reply, decision, committee report, director approval, published version, revision dates, private attachments. Advisory members receive only explicitly authorized document or summary, not entire private library. Preserve earlier versions and external disclosure audit.

3. Build bilingual responsive UI with registration, grant form, scoped workspaces, member registry, department charter and six-month follow-up, director calendar and committee.
4. Add end-to-end tests for staff vs centers, center A vs B, HQ ungranted vs granted, expiry/revocation, mentor isolation, private photo access, committee approval, financial data and data preservation. Existing tests must pass.
5. Review deployment and live test Cloudflare, Pages, PWA cache, desktop and iPhone before merge. New tests in this branch have not been run yet.

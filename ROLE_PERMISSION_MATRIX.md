# Role permissions

Reporting roles: six department leaders, Administrative and Finance Staff, Monday Staff Program Leader, Country Director, International Director.

Non-reporting roles: International Staff, Assisting Staff, Prayer Staff. These roles cannot see Dashboard, Reports, report comments, or New Report; they can access attendance, Monday-program content, announcements, assigned projects, directory, and profile.

Only Country Director and International Director can see the document registry and add/edit Director comments.
## Delegation recipients (updated 2026-11-25)

Only a Country or International Director can grant or revoke permissions and delegations; a recipient cannot pass them on. **Any active member except a Director can now be a recipient.** What may be granted depends on the recipient's role class:

| Recipient | Modules | Actions | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HQ department leader | all governance modules | view, create, edit, approve | organization, country, international or one center |
| Staff (HQ, international, assisting, prayer) | operational modules (no member registry, mentorship, succession, advisory board, director visits) | view, create, edit | as above |
| Center senior leader, center leader, center department leader | own-center modules: center registry, center plans, center reports, center attendance, documents, teaching, member registry, mentorship | view, create, edit, approve | **own center only** |
| Potential leaders, volunteers | center registry, center plans, center reports, center attendance, documents, teaching | view, create, edit | **own center only** |

Center members can never be delegated HQ plans or reports, Monday attendance, director visits, succession or the advisory board, and a center account cannot read HQ-scope records even with a grant. A center member's grant stops working if they move to another center.
